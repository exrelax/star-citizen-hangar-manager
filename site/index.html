What it does

Everything RSI's hangar pages show – and what they don't.

1

Your whole hangar, instantly

No more paging through RSI's 10 pledges per page.

  • Opens instantly from local data
  • Searches thousands of pledges as you type: by title, ship, pledge ID or order ID
  • Filters that include or exclude ships, CCUs, paints, LTI, warbond, giftable and more
2

Buy back with confidence

See what you get back before you spend a token.

  • Buy back pledges straight into the RSI cart
  • Your buy back tokens at a glance
  • Hints which buy backs are probably giftable, plus links to erkul.games and FleetYards.net
3

Every upgrade, every discount

The story of every upgraded ship, step by step.

  • Discounts of ships and CCUs compared to their original price
  • The full CCU history, with the discount of every step
  • Orders with invoices, a refund countdown and a prefilled refund request
4

Made to fit in

An addition to RSI's pages, not a replacement.

  • Two themes: Modern Hangar and Classic RSI
  • Three font sizes and a compact view
  • In English, German, French and Spanish
5

Private by design

Your hangar stays in your browser.

  • No account, no server of its own, no tracking, no analytics
  • Smart updates check a few RSI pages instead of reading the whole hangar again
  • Hide IDs, your name, values and refund periods for screenshots and streams
