Getting started

  1. 1

    Install the extension

    From your browser's store above.

  2. 2

    Log in on the RSI website

    The Hangar Manager uses your RSI session, so you need to be logged in on robertsspaceindustries.com to use it.

  3. 3

    Open the Hangar Manager

    The new first entry “Hangar Manager” in your account navigation, above “My Gear”.

Open the Hangar Manager

Also from the extension's icon in the browser toolbar (pin it from the extensions menu first).

What it does

Everything RSI's hangar pages show – and what they don't.

1

Your whole hangar, instantly

No more paging through RSI's 10 pledges per page.

  • Opens instantly from local data
  • Searches thousands of pledges as you type: by title, ship, pledge ID or order ID
  • Filters that include or exclude ships, CCUs, paints, LTI, warbond, giftable and more
2

Buy back with confidence

See what you get back before you spend a token.

  • Buy back pledges straight into the RSI cart
  • Your buy back tokens at a glance
  • Hints which buy backs are probably giftable, plus links to erkul.games and FleetYards.net
3

The Pledge Store at a glance

Warbond CCUs, events and price increases in one place.

  • The current warbond CCUs with the cheapest ships to start from
  • A history of the warbond offers seen so far, with their events
  • Event notices like the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo, with countdowns
4

More store credit from your paints

The Paint Discount Consultant uses RSI's volume discount.

  • Shows which paints to melt and buy again, on two ways
  • A plan you start and follow step by step: cart, paying, melting
  • Paid with store credit only – never with new money
5

Ahead of every price increase

Know what gets more expensive before it does.

  • The cheapest CCUs to secure before a ship's price rises
  • A reminder on buy back items that get more expensive
  • Advice for CCUs that lose or gain upgrade value

How it works

The Hangar Manager reads your hangar, buy back and orders from the RSI website with the session you're already logged in with. Everything is stored only in your browser: there is no server of its own, no account and no tracking.

RSI's own “My Gear” and “Buy Back Pledges” pages stay as they are, and nothing is bought, melted or gifted without your click.
