Hangar & Buy Back

Thousands of pledges. One clear list.

Your whole hangar and buy back on one page instead of RSI’s 10 pledges per page – found as you type.

  • Search by title, ship, pledge or order ID
  • Filters that include or exclude: ships, CCUs, paints, LTI, warbond, giftable, meltable, discount …
  • Sort by date, value or discount – with melt values, discounts and the full CCU history

Price increases

Don’t lose a discount you already paid for.

When a ship gets more expensive, CCUs from it lose upgrade value. The manager warns you in time – and shows how to keep the discount.

  • Warnings on CCUs that lose value, with a countdown to the increase
  • A swap recommendation: melt them and buy CCUs to the rising ship with the store credit they give back – e.g. Prospector → Vulture becomes Freelancer MAX → Prospector
  • Buy back reminders for items about to cost more

Paint Discount Consultant

Up to 35% on your paints – with store credit only.

RSI gives up to 35% on 10 paints in one cart. The consultant melts your paints together, buys them again with cheap filler paints, and melts the fillers after 24 hours.

  • Two ways – buy first (safe) or melt first (more store credit) – compared side by side
  • A plan you start and follow step by step: cart, paying, melting
  • Checks your cart and store credit, and counts down RSI’s 24 hours for the fillers

Buy Back

See which buy backs you can still gift.

RSI only lets you gift a buy back that was giftable when you melted it. The manager works it out from your order history and RSI’s new gift rules.

  • Marked “Probably giftable” right in the buy back list and as a filter
  • For pledges melted from now on, RSI’s own gift flag is kept
  • Buy back pledges straight into the RSI cart – with their buy back price
Probably giftable
  • Bought before Oct 30, 2026 – RSI’s new gift rules start then
  • Paid with money only – no store credit, from your order history
  • Giftable when melted – RSI keeps that status for the buy back

Ready in a minute.

The first load reads your hangar, buy back and orders once – about 20 seconds for 1,000 pledges. From then on it opens instantly.

Chrome Web Store Firefox Add-ons
  1. 1Install the extensionFrom the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons. Opera and Edge install it from a release file.
  2. 2Log in on the RSI websiteThe manager reads your hangar with your own RSI session – it never sees your password.
  3. 3Open “Hangar Manager”The new first entry in your account navigation, above “My Gear” – or from the toolbar icon. Open it on the RSI website.
