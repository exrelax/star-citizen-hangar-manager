## Description

```text
Star Citizen Hangar Manager turns your hangar on robertsspaceindustries.com into one fast, searchable page – right on the RSI website, in its look and feel.

BUILT FOR SPEED
• Opens instantly from local data – no more paging through RSI's 10 pledges per page
• Searches thousands of pledges as you type: by title, ship, pledge ID or order ID
• Smart updates find changes within seconds by checking a few RSI pages in the background, instead of reading the whole hangar again
• Search, filter and switch tabs while it refreshes – the manager never blocks you

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no server of its own, no tracking, no analytics
• Your hangar, buy back and orders are stored only in your browser
• Browser notifications only if you turn them on – the permission is asked for then, never at install
• Hide pledge and order IDs, your name and address, values and refund periods for screenshots and streams – or show an anonymized selection in the demo mode

YOUR WHOLE HANGAR AND BUY BACK
• Filters that include or exclude: ships, CCUs, paints, game access, rewards, LTI, warbond, giftable, meltable, valuable, discount
• The best insurance of every pledge as a tag (LTI, 10 years, …)
• Discounts of ships and CCUs compared to their original price
• The full CCU history of upgraded ships, with the discount of every step
• Orders with invoices, a refund countdown and a prefilled refund request
• Buy back pledges straight into the RSI cart, and your buy back tokens at a glance
• Exchange (melt) and gift dialogs that make clear what you are about to give up
• Links to erkul.games and FleetYards.net, and from CCU Game (ccugame.app) straight to the pledge

AN ADDITION, NOT A REPLACEMENT
RSI's own "My Gear" and "Buy Back Pledges" pages stay as they are. The manager adds an entry to the account navigation, and nothing is bought, melted or gifted without your click.

Two themes (Modern Hangar and Classic RSI), three font sizes and a compact view. In English, German, French and Spanish.

Open source: https://github.com/exrelax/star-citizen-hangar-manager

```
