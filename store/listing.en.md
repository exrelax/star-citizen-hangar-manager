## Description

```text
Star Citizen Hangar Manager turns your hangar on robertsspaceindustries.com into one fast, searchable page – right on the RSI website, in its look and feel.

BUILT FOR SPEED
• Opens instantly from local data – no more paging through RSI's 10 pledges per page
• Searches thousands of pledges as you type: by title, ship, pledge ID or order ID
• Smart updates find changes within seconds by checking a few RSI pages in the background, instead of reading the whole hangar again
• Search, filter and switch tabs while it refreshes – the manager never blocks you

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no server of its own, no tracking, no analytics
• Your hangar, buy back and orders are stored only in your browser
• Browser notifications only if you turn them on – the permission is asked for then, never at install
• Hide pledge and order IDs, your name and address, values and refund periods for screenshots and streams – or show an anonymized selection in the demo mode
• Watchlists, marks to melt and paint plans follow your browser profile to your other devices, through your browser's own sync
• Export your hangar as JSON, as CSV or for FleetYards (HangarXPLOR format), and back up your settings, watchlists and plans

YOUR WHOLE HANGAR AND BUY BACK
• Filters that include or exclude: ships, CCUs, paints, game access, rewards, LTI, warbond, giftable, meltable, valuable, discount – refined right in the filter: solo ships or ship packs, CCUs without base ship, paints for ships you own
• A grouped view: equal pledges in one row, with their count and sums
• List shortcuts like "Ready to melt", "Paints to optimize" and "Melt and rebuy"
• The best insurance of every pledge as a tag (LTI, 10 years, …)
• Discounts of ships and CCUs compared to their original price
• The full CCU history of upgraded ships, with the discount of every step
• Orders with invoices, a refund countdown and a prefilled refund request
• Buy back pledges straight into the RSI cart, with their buy back price, and your buy back tokens with the date of their next renewal
• Sorting by date, value or discount; select pledges, or all at once, and see their summary
• Mark pledges to melt later, also all selected ones at once, and get told when RSI's 24 hours are over
• Melt and Rebuy: the pledges you can melt now and buy again later at the same price, with a watchlist to rebuy them
• Exchange (melt) and gift dialogs that make clear what you are about to give up
• Links to erkul.games and FleetYards.net, and from CCU Game (ccugame.app) straight to the pledge

CCU CHECK
• Which of your CCUs have a base ship to start from: in your hangar, only via your buy back, by a ship the store always sells, or none
• A simple view with a card per base ship, and a deep view that follows whole CCU chains: which ways are missing and what securing them costs in store credit
• CCUs soon without base ship when a ship leaves the store, and CCUs whose discount is at risk before a price increase
• Counts of your ships and CCUs right in RSI's ship upgrade dialog

WATCHLISTS
• Collect pledges, buy back items and Pledge Store items under a name of your choice, with a date and a reminder
• "Put on a watchlist" buttons on RSI's own store pages and in its ship upgrade dialog
• Notifications when a watched store item gets a new price or goes on or off sale, and the cart filled from a watchlist

THE PLEDGE STORE AT A GLANCE
• The current warbond CCUs with price and discount, the cheapest ships to start from and your own CCUs in their price range, sorted by when they are expected to end
• All ships by value in one table, with warbond prices, price increases and your CCUs from and to each ship
• Ships leaving the Pledge Store, with a countdown and a warning on the CCUs that would lose their base ship
• A history of the warbond offers seen so far, with their events
• Event notices like the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo, with countdowns
• Expected price increases: a reminder on buy back items that get more expensive, advice for CCUs that lose or gain upgrade value, and the CCUs to secure before a ship's price rises
• Paint Discount Planner: more store credit from your paints with RSI's volume discount, as a plan you start and follow step by step, and a note in RSI's cart when its paints miss the best discount
• Notifications in the sidebar about Pledge Store changes, events, price increases, ships leaving the store, watchlists and pledges ready to melt

AN ADDITION, NOT A REPLACEMENT
RSI's own "My Gear" and "Buy Back Pledges" pages stay as they are. The manager adds an entry to the account navigation, and nothing is bought, melted or gifted without your click. Bulk melting is experimental and off by default: it asks before you turn it on, melts only a set number of pledges at once and checks each of them with RSI right before.

Two themes (Modern Hangar and Classic RSI), three font sizes and a compact view. In English, German, French and Spanish.

Open source: https://github.com/exrelax/star-citizen-hangar-manager
Built with the help of AI (Claude Code), every change reviewed and tested. The extension itself uses no AI and sends nothing to an AI service.

```
